Vuemio Stream Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Vuemio Stream is an IPTV media player and electronic programme guide for Apple TV. This privacy policy outlines what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

Vuemio Stream does not collect, sell, track, or share your personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics frameworks, no telemetry tracking, and no advertising. Vuemio Stream operates with no proprietary backend server — nothing you watch, search, or configure is ever transmitted to us.

What stays on your Apple TV

All of your configuration and activity is stored strictly locally on your Apple TV:

  • Playlist Credentials: Xtream Codes API server addresses, usernames, and passwords are encrypted and stored in the secure Apple TV Keychain.
  • Playlists & EPG: Channel lists, categories, and electronic programme guide listings are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database.
  • Watch History & Favourites: Continue Watching playback positions, marked favourite channels, and recent streams are kept locally on your device.
  • Preferences: Tile sizing, audio/subtitle preferences, and player buffer settings.

You can erase all stored data and credentials at any time using Settings → General → Reset All Settings to Defaults, or by deleting the app from your Apple TV.

Connections the app makes

To function as a media player, Vuemio Stream makes direct outgoing connections from your Apple TV:

  • Your IPTV Provider: When loading playlists, EPG listings, or streaming video, your Apple TV connects directly to your provider's server. As with any direct network request, your provider will see your IP address and stream requests according to their own terms and privacy policy.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB): To display movie and series posters, backdrops, and metadata, Vuemio Stream queries TMDB directly via secure HTTPS (api.themoviedb.org and image.tmdb.org). No personal account data or playlist credentials are ever shared with TMDB.

Video Streaming and Content

Vuemio Stream is a media player only. It does not host, provide, or distribute media streams. Video playback is decoded and rendered locally on your device hardware using Apple's AVPlayer and mpv rendering. Streams are never proxied or recorded by Vuemio Stream.

Purchases and Pricing

Vuemio Stream is 100% free to download and use on tvOS. There are no paid tiers, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Children

Vuemio Stream does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users.
