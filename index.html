tvOS Media Player

Vuemio Stream

A fast, native IPTV player and guide for Apple TV.

Vuemio Stream brings your IPTV playlists, live channels with electronic programme guide (EPG), catchup playback, movies, and TV shows together into a smooth, native Apple TV interface.

100% Free: Vuemio Stream is completely free to download and use on tvOS. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no advertisements.

Bring Your Own Playlist: Vuemio Stream does not provide, host, or distribute any content or video streams. Connect using your Xtream Codes API or M3U playlist credentials. All playlist data and credentials are stored strictly on your Apple TV.

Vuemio Stream uses The Movie Database (TMDB) API for artwork and metadata.
This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
