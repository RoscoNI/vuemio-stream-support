Common Questions

Is Vuemio Stream free?

Yes. Vuemio Stream is 100% free to download and use on tvOS. There are no paid tiers, no mandatory subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no advertisements.

Does Vuemio Stream include channels or streams?

No. Vuemio Stream is purely a media player and interface. It does not provide, host, license, or distribute any media content or video streams. You must provide your own legitimate playlist from your IPTV provider.

How do I connect my playlist?

Launch Vuemio Stream on your Apple TV and navigate to the initial setup screen or Settings → Playlists. You can log in using your Xtream Codes API server address, username, and password, or load an M3U playlist URL. Credentials are saved securely on your Apple TV Keychain.

Where does movie and TV show artwork come from?

Movie and TV show information, high-resolution posters, backdrops, and metadata are retrieved from The Movie Database (TMDB).

Vuemio Stream uses The Movie Database (TMDB) API for artwork and metadata.
This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Why is artwork or a synopsis missing?

Metadata is matched by title. If your provider uses non-standard naming conventions or release tags in the title, TMDB may not locate a matching title automatically. We are continuously improving matching heuristics.

How does Catchup work?

If your IPTV provider supports catchup archives (via Xtream Codes API), past programmes in the Electronic Programme Guide will display a catchup indicator. Selecting that programme will start playback from your provider's archive.

How do I reset or clear all data?

Open Settings → General → Reset All Settings to Defaults. This completely clears all saved playlists, cached guide data, watch history, and preferences on this Apple TV.
